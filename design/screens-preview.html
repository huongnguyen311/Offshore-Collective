M04 · Core
Booking Calendar
Signature Moment #3: rounded-square date glyphs echo the brand mark; boat identity reuses Home's header. Six states: available, booked, blocked, standby, unclaimed, and named-holiday long weekend. They are stepped by lightness and each light state carries a non-colour cue (solid border, dashed border, no border, hatch, TODAY label), because no light tint reaches 3:1 against the others. Past and beyond-the-60-day-window share one muted not-bookable look. A named holiday long weekend is carried by a 3px rule across all four cells rather than by fill, which says Fri to Mon is one indivisible unit (C.4); a holiday that formed no long weekend that year takes no cell treatment and is named in the key as a single day. The corner glyph marks only dates the partner cannot select, so booked and blocked keep it and standby and unclaimed do not.
